⭒｡꩜˚Jojo‘s Garden˚꩜｡⭒

Whenever I want to be in my garden I just close my eyes. The noice around me cancels out. All I can hear now are birds singing, a brease gently combing through grass and people laughing in the distance. When I open my eyes again I am in the shire. I can see little Hobbits running after Gandalf the Grey expecting fireworks. I start to wander through my garden. I fight orgs, trolls and dragons following the a mossy path through my garden. Another path appears in sight. The ground turns from moss to stone pathment and suddenly I am in the city. There is a runaway boy, running from a butcher with red hair and blue eyes. I follow him on his journey until he wins the game. Then I have to leave but a new path opens. Suddenly someone calls my name and I am back in my room. I have to leave my garden for the "real world" but I will return to live another life yet again.
